# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Vue 3 (Composition API) + Vite + Tailwind CSS

## Users

University students, faculty, staff, and campus visitors who have lost a personal belonging or found an unattended item on campus and want to safely reunite it with its owner.

## Product Purpose

LostStuff2 is a community-driven lost-and-found platform that makes reporting, discovering, filtering, and claiming lost and found items fast, reliable, and effortless. Success means high recovery rates, minimal reporting friction, and clear, safe communication between finders and owners.

## Positioning

A modern, high-trust, and visually intuitive lost-and-found hub with categorized listings, location tagging, smart match suggestions, and privacy-conscious claim verification—replacing messy social media group posts and fragmented physical notice boards.

## Operating Context

Everyday urban, transit, campus, cafe, and event environments where items are misplaced or discovered. Users often access the platform on mobile or desktop during moments of urgency, stress, or helpful community action.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Report lost items with image uploads/previews, descriptions, categories, timestamps, and locations.
- Report found items with safe pickup/contact instructions.
- Search and multi-filter system (by status, category, date range, location proximity).
- Automated or suggested match indicators between lost and found entries.
- Secure claim request and contact workflow to protect user privacy and prevent false claims.
- Responsive, accessible, and fast web application.

## Brand Commitments

- **Palette Theme**: Warm White & Brown (Warm Alabaster / Clean Paper White `#FAFAF8`, Warm Cream / Oatmeal `#F5F0EB`, Rich Espresso / Cocoa `#2D2016` typography, Warm Chestnut / Caramel `#784D34` & `#A26A4A` primary buttons & borders, Soft Amber `#D97706` for Lost, Soft Olive / Sage `#15803D` for Found/Returned).
- **Tone**: Empathic, trustworthy, warm, clean, and community-spirited. Clear visual hierarchy with intuitive status markers (Lost vs. Found vs. Reunited).

## Product Principles

- **Frictionless Reporting**: Help stressed users post or search an item in seconds.
- **Trust & Safe Return**: Protect personal contact info while facilitating verified ownership recovery.
- **High Scannability**: Visual cues, color-coded badges, and clear imagery enable rapid browsing.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast standards, keyboard navigation, clear aria attributes, and mobile-first responsive layout.
