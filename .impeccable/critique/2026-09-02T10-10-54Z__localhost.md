- **LLM Assessment**: High specificity. The visual language (Warm White & Brown), campus building taxonomy (Central Library, SC Complex, Canteen C1, Sports Gym, Student Union), and the weighted Automated Matching Engine are deeply tailored to the university context rather than being generic.

### Overall Impression
The application feels warm, reassuring, highly functional, and visually distinctive. The color palette successfully transforms a stressful lost-item scenario into an empathetic, community-supported recovery workflow.

---

### What's Working
1. **Automated Matching Engine & Instant Alert Modal**: Side-by-side comparison with score breakdown and percentage badge delivers immediate user reassurance.
2. **Warm White & Brown Palette**: Clean paper white ground with oatmeal cream and espresso typography creates an inviting, accessible atmosphere.
3. **Bilingual Toggle & Filter Hub**: Smooth real-time switching between Thai and English with category chips and building dropdowns.

### Priority Issues

- **[P2] Replace browser native `alert()` dialogs with custom in-app Toast notifications**:
  - *Why it matters*: Native alerts interrupt user flow and feel unstyled.
  - *Fix*: Implement an animated Toast notification container for claim submissions and report confirmations.
  - *Suggested command*: `/impeccable polish`

- **[P2] Add direct camera / local image file upload alongside image URL presets**:
  - *Why it matters*: Mobile users on campus taking a photo of a found item need direct file upload.
  - *Fix*: Add `<input type="file" accept="image/*">` with `FileReader` preview.
  - *Suggested command*: `/impeccable harden`

- **[P3] Add search keyboard shortcut (`/` or `Ctrl+K`)**:
  - *Why it matters*: Power users can instantly focus the search bar from anywhere on the page.
  - *Fix*: Add global keydown listener for `/` or `Ctrl+K`.
  - *Suggested command*: `/impeccable delight`

### Persona Red Flags
